Abstract

Have you ever noticed when a digital character’s face just looks... wrong? Human eyes are highly sensitive to fake expressions. Creating realistic digital faces is a massive challenge: traditional animation offers artistic freedom but often lacks physical realism, while true physical simulations are incredibly complex, mathematically heavy, and painfully slow to compute. Our research bridges this gap. We have developed a digital "musculoskeletal" system that takes the heavy lifting out of biomechanics. Instead of wrestling with dense equations and long rendering times, animators and researchers can intuitively "play" with our model like a digital puppet. By automatically translating simple artistic controls into complex, underlying muscle movements, our tool allows creators to generate highly realistic, physically accurate facial expressions—including speech. We are putting the power of advanced physics directly into the hands of artists.

Keywords: Digital Puppeteering, Facial Animation, Biomechanics, Physics, Computer Graphics.
